# Product vision

## One-line positioning

**An AI-native risk intelligence layer for global trading portfolios.**

Novera does not replace trading systems, pricing libraries or the official risk engine.
It sits above them and turns risk measurements into investigation, explanation, scenario
analysis, exception management and action.

## Why this and not "another risk system"

Banks and hedge funds already own risk engines, internally or through vendors such as
Murex, Calypso, Numerix, MSCI RiskManager, Bloomberg PORT, Imagine and Beacon. They do
not lack numbers. They lack a layer that answers, every morning and under stress:

| Question                                   | Traditional stack | Novera |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------------|--------|
| What is my risk?                           | Yes               | Yes (own engine, or ingested) |
| What changed since yesterday?              | Partly            | Yes, automatically |
| Why did it change?                         | Manual            | Attributed and explained |
| Can I trust today's numbers?               | Rarely explicit   | Data-quality module |
| What could go wrong from here?             | Manual stress     | Scenario engine + suggested scenarios |
| Which limits, who owns them, what next?    | Spreadsheets      | Breach workflow |
| Where do two engines disagree, and why?    | Ad hoc            | Independent challenger |

The commercial wedge is therefore aggregation, reconciliation, explainability, control
workflow and governance, with AI as the interface. Multi-asset coverage is proof of
breadth, not the moat.

## Seven capabilities

1. **Risk aggregation** across trades, books, desks, businesses, legal entities, firm.
2. **Risk monitoring**: day-on-day change detection at every level.
3. **Risk investigation**: P&L and risk attribution to market moves, trading, model and data.
4. **Risk simulation**: historical, hypothetical and cross-asset stress; what-if trades.
5. **Risk control**: limit hierarchy, utilisation, breach lifecycle, escalation, audit.
6. **Risk intelligence**: ranked material risks, concentrations, liquidity and early warnings.
7. **Novera Analyst**: natural-language access via tool calls to the engine and database.

## Two audiences, one engine

| Investment bank face                       | Hedge fund face |
|--------------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Legal-entity and desk aggregation          | Multi-prime-broker aggregation |
| Limit governance and escalation            | Fund-level limits and IC reporting |
| Counterparty exposure, collateral, CVA     | Prime-broker exposure and margin |
| Capital: FRTB, SA-CCR, RWA                 | Leverage, gross/net, factor exposures |
| Regulatory evidence and audit trail        | Liquidity, redemption stress, crowding |
| Independent challenge of official engine   | Strategy attribution |

## Flagship demonstrations

1. **Morning risk dashboard** with drill-down firm to trade, and automatic day-on-day explain.
2. **Global risk-off stress** with losses by desk, breaches raised, top contributors.
3. **Independent challenger**: two engines disagree on VaR; the platform attributes the gap
   to market-data timestamp, portfolio scope, model version, risk-factor mapping or error.
4. **Can I trust today's VaR?** Data-quality report: stale surfaces, missing curve nodes,
   unpriced trades, unexplained P&L.
5. **Counterparty what-if**: change a CSA threshold, re-run exposure, compare runs.
6. **Novera Analyst**: "Why did VaR increase?" answered from sourced numbers with run IDs.

## Claims we make

> The platform complements existing risk engines by making multi-source risk results
> consistent, explainable and operationally actionable.

## Claims we avoid

- A complete replacement for bank risk systems.
- Better pricing than established vendors.
- Coverage of every product from day one.
- AI that calculates regulatory risk.
- Fully automated risk management with no human accountability.

## Owner's goals served

- Hiring as market risk lead: the platform, its methodology docs and the demo prove depth.
- Startup path: the intelligence-layer positioning is sellable first to small and mid-size
  hedge funds, later to banks as an independent control layer.
