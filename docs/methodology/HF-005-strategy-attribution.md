## Definition
Per strategy: PV, gross and net exposure as % of NAV, component VaR and share of fund
VaR (MR-002), today's P&L (MR-007), and statistics of the hypothetical scenario P&L
series: annualised return and volatility as % of NAV, a Sharpe-like ratio with zero
funding, maximum drawdown of the cumulative series.
